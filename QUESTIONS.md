# Performance Passport Questions

This document captures the coaching questions that Performance Passport aims to answer.

Every major feature should answer one or more of these questions.

If a proposed feature does not help answer a coaching question, it should be challenged before development.

---

# Today's Run

## Session Quality

- How good was this run, really?
- Was this one of my best runs of this type?
- How did this compare with my normal performance?
- What made this run better or worse than usual?
- Was today's performance affected by the conditions?

---

## Efficiency

- Was I more aerobically efficient than normal?
- Did I run faster for the same heart rate?
- Was my heart rate unusually high or low?
- Is my running economy improving?

---

## Context

- How much did heat affect today's run?
- How much did elevation affect today's run?
- How much did terrain affect today's run?
- How much did fatigue affect today's performance?
- Was today's run harder than it appeared?

---

# Training Progress

## Fitness

- Am I getting fitter?
- Is my aerobic fitness improving?
- Is my threshold improving?
- Am I becoming more durable?
- Is my running economy improving?

---

## Training Load

- Am I carrying fatigue?
- Am I recovering well?
- Am I training too hard?
- Am I training enough?
- Is my current training load sustainable?

---

## Training Effectiveness

- Which sessions produce the biggest improvements?
- Which training blocks have been my most successful?
- What weekly mileage suits me best?
- What balance of easy and hard running works best for me?
- Which sessions should I do more often?
- Which sessions are giving me little benefit?

---

# Race Preparation

## Preparation

- What preparation has produced my best races?
- What does my ideal 12-week race build-up look like?
- What taper works best for me?
- How many easy days should I take before racing?
- What long-run pattern produces my best performances?
- What weekly mileage gives me my best race results?

---

## Readiness

- Am I ready to race?
- When should I race?
- Should I train harder or recover?
- Am I likely to run a personal best?
- What race time is realistic today?

---

# Long-Term Development

## Trends

- Am I improving faster than before?
- Where have I made the biggest improvements?
- What is limiting my progress?
- When was I at my fittest?
- What has changed over the last year?

---

## Injuries

- What training patterns lead to niggles or injuries?
- Which shoes reduce injury risk?
- Does fatigue increase my injury risk?
- Am I returning safely after time off?

---

# Equipment

- Which shoes suit me best?
- Which shoes are fastest?
- Which shoes improve my efficiency?
- When should I retire a pair of shoes?

---

# Passport Insights

Ultimately, Performance Passport should be able to answer:

> "How good was this run compared with similar runs I have done before, after accounting for the conditions I ran in?"

Every future coaching insight should help answer that question.
